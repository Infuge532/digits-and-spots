Insurance & payment

Fees & financial information

Digits & Spots is a self-pay practice. Orofacial myofunctional therapy is generally not covered by dental or medical insurance, though some plans may offer partial reimbursement.

Self-pay

Fees are collected at the time of service. Kathleen will discuss costs with you at or before your initial evaluation.

HSA & FSA accepted

Health Savings Account and Flexible Spending Account funds are accepted for all services.

Insurance documentation

Kathleen can provide itemized superbills for you to submit to your insurance carrier for possible out-of-network reimbursement.

Professional organizations

Where to learn more about OMT

International Association of Orofacial Myology (IAOM)
The leading credentialing body for Certified Orofacial Myologists worldwide. Find research, CE resources, and locate practitioners.
American Speech-Language-Hearing Association (ASHA)
National organization for communication and swallowing disorders, with resources on orofacial myofunctional disorders and treatment.
Buteyko Clinic International
Patrick McKeown's organization for Buteyko breathing education, research, and certified instructor training.
Upledger Institute International
The source for craniosacral therapy training and research, including applications for infant and pediatric care.
MNRI — Masgutova Neurosensorimotor Reflex Integration
The Svetlana Masgutova Educational Institute's method for integrating retained primitive reflexes — an approach Kathleen incorporates into her practice.
Suggested reading

Books Kathleen recommends

Jaws: The Story of a Hidden Epidemic — Sandra Kahn & Paul R. Ehrlich
A compelling look at how modern diets and mouth breathing have altered human facial development over generations.
Breath: The New Science of a Lost Art — James Nestor
An accessible and eye-opening exploration of how we breathe and why nasal breathing matters for health and development.
The Oxygen Advantage — Patrick McKeown
How breathing less can improve athletic performance, sleep quality, and overall health — from the creator of Buteyko Breathing.
